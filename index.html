Our Process

01 / How it works

From your product to the field, without a new factory.

  1. Clients

    Client hardware

    Drone makers and other defense hardware companies bring an existing product.

  2. Suho

    Kit design

    We redesign the product into simple modules built on general-purpose machines.

  3. Network

    Garage factories

    Design files go out to many garages. Each makes parts and packs kits.

  4. Field

    Kits delivered

    Compact kits ship to where they’re needed and are assembled without special tools.

02 / Example

A drone company wants to scale up.

The client
A drone company with a proven airframe and growing orders.
The problem
Scaling up means a bigger factory, specialized tooling, and skilled workers, which takes years and a lot of capital.
The ask
More drones, sooner, without building their own new production line.
Step 01

Suho redesigns the drone as a kit.

Break it into modules
Wings, fuselage, tail, and an electronics bay.
Design for simple machines
Every part can be cut, printed, or foam-formed on general-purpose machines. No welding or precision machining.
Build in the instructions
Parts only fit one way, with jigs and step-by-step guides, so assembly takes no special skills.
Step 02

Garage factories make the kits.

Files go out
Suho sends the kit design files to garages across the network.
Same machines everywhere
Each garage runs the same cutter, 3D printers, and foam cutter, so any garage can make any kit.
Built-in checks
Go/no-go jigs and standard checks keep every garage’s parts consistent.
Packed as kits
Parts are packed into compact kits, ready to ship.
