How it works

From a flat sheet to a flying drone in three steps.

Photo: flat-packed kit
STEP 1

Unpack

The airframe arrives as a single flat cardboard sheet, pre-cut and scored.

Photo: folding the frame
STEP 2

Fold

Fold along the lines and lock the tabs. No glue or tools needed.

Photo: ready to fly
STEP 3

Fly

Drop in the motors, battery and flight controller, then take off.

Why cardboard?

It sounds unusual. It turns out to be a very good material for small airframes.

Low cost

Airframes cost a fraction of molded or carbon frames.

Ships flat

Dozens of frames fit in the space of one assembled drone.

Light

Corrugated board is stiff for its weight, which means longer flights.

Recyclable

Crashed or worn out frames go straight into the recycling bin.
